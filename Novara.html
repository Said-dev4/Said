Brand Guidelines

Usage rules

Consistency protects the brand. Follow these rules in every context where the identity appears.

✓ Do
  • Use the logo on approved backgrounds only
  • Maintain clear space around the logo mark
  • Use the defined color palette exclusively
  • Keep the logo proportional — never stretch it
  • Pair typefaces as specified in the font system
✕ Don't
  • Add drop shadows or effects to the logo
  • Use off-brand colors or gradients
  • Place the logo on busy or cluttered backgrounds
  • Mix more than two typefaces in one layout
  • Alter, recreate, or rearrange logo elements
